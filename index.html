Key takeaways

  • Cloud service models differ in what the provider manages.
  • Virtualization helps allocate compute, memory, and storage efficiently.
  • Security is a shared responsibility between provider and customer.
  • SLAs, risk planning, monitoring, and backups support reliability.
  • Scaling capacity to demand can improve performance and control cost.
